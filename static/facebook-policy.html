Facebook Messenger Policy

When can you send
broadcast messages?

Facebook enforces a strict messaging window. Understanding it explains why some broadcasts succeed and others fail — and exactly what to do about it.

At a glance

⚡
Within 24 Hours
Contact sent your Page a message recently. Any reply is allowed — no special permissions needed.
🔒
Outside 24h, Unreviewed App
All message types are blocked by Facebook. No workaround exists without Meta App Review.
🚀
Outside 24h, Approved App
MESSAGE_TAG unlocks proactive broadcasts to any opted-in contact, anytime.

How to fix this permanently Recommended Path

1
Go to developers.facebook.com and open your app's dashboard.
2
Navigate to App Review → Permissions and Features. Find pages_messaging_subscriptions and click Request.
3
Provide a screen recording of your broadcast flow, a privacy policy URL, and a description of your use case. Submit for review.
4
Once approved, Magolis will automatically use MESSAGE_TAG with CONFIRMED_EVENT_UPDATE — broadcasts will reach all opted-in contacts regardless of when they last messaged.
💡
Quick workaround while waiting for approval: Ask your contacts to send any message to your Facebook Page (e.g. "Hi"), then run your broadcast within 24 hours. Their message opens the window and RESPONSE type will work immediately.
